Privacy Policy

WakeTok Support

WakeTok lets you swipe up and down through the photos and videos in your own library, full screen. It has no account and never uploads your photos.

Photo access

WakeTok needs access to your photo library to work. With limited access, only the items you selected are shown, and albums and some features need full access. To change access, open Settings in WakeTok and use Photo Library Access, or go to iOS Settings > Apps > WakeTok > Photos.

The WakeTok album

Swipe left on an item to add it to the "WakeTok" album in Photos, as a bookmark to find it later. Nothing is moved or deleted. You can turn this gesture off in WakeTok Settings > Gestures.

Deleting

When you delete an item in WakeTok, iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos.

Location when sharing

Photos are shared without their location information; Live Photos are shared as a still photo. Videos are shared as the original file and keep their original metadata, including the location where they were recorded, if present.

Your data

WakeTok keeps its settings and Saved Pools on your device only. Deleting WakeTok removes them; your photos and the WakeTok album stay in Photos.
