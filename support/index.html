Privacy Policy

WakeTok Support

WakeTok lets you swipe up and down through the photos and videos in your own library, full screen. There is no account, and WakeTok never uploads your photos or videos.

Photo access

WakeTok needs access to your photo library to work. With limited access, WakeTok shows only the items you selected; albums and some features need full access. To change access, open Settings in WakeTok and use Photo Library Access, or change WakeTok's Photos access in iOS Settings.

The WakeTok album

Swipe left on an item to add it to the "WakeTok" album in Photos, as a bookmark to find it later. Nothing is moved or deleted. You can turn this gesture off in WakeTok Settings > Gestures.

Deleting

When you delete an item in WakeTok, iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos.

Location when sharing

Still photos are shared without their location information. Live Photos are shared as a still photo. Videos are shared as Photos provides them and may retain their metadata, including the location where they were recorded, if present.

Your data

WakeTok stores your settings, Saved Pools, and other app data on your device and does not upload them to me. If you back up your device, the backup may include this data. To delete the data WakeTok stores on your device, delete the app; your photos and the WakeTok album stay in Photos. Device backups made before you delete the app may still contain copies; those backups are managed by iOS and your backup settings, not by WakeTok.
