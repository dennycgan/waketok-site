Photo access

WakeTok needs access to your photo library to work. With limited access, WakeTok shows only the photos and videos you selected; albums and some features need full access. To change access, open Settings in WakeTok and choose Photo Library Access, or change WakeTok's Photos access in iOS Settings.

The WakeTok album

Swipe left on an item to add it to the "WakeTok" album in Photos, as a bookmark to find it later. Nothing is moved or deleted. You can turn this gesture off in WakeTok Settings > Gestures.

Deleting

When you delete an item in WakeTok, iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos.

Sharing and location

When you share an item, WakeTok opens the iOS share sheet. Still photos are shared without location information. Live Photos are shared as a still photo. Videos may retain metadata, including recording location. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Your data

To delete the data WakeTok stores on your device, delete the app; your photos and the WakeTok album stay in Photos. See the Privacy Policy for details about backups.
