WakeTok Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Summary

WakeTok does not collect any data. It does not send your photos, videos, or activity to us or to anyone else; the only time WakeTok sends anything off your device is when you choose to share it. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. WakeTok has no server, and the app includes no analytics or advertising tools.

What WakeTok accesses

With your permission, WakeTok reads the photos, videos, and Live Photos in your photo library on your device to show them full screen. You can allow full access or limited access to selected items, and change this at any time in iOS Settings. WakeTok asks for no other permissions.

If you use iCloud Photos and an original is not stored on your device, iOS may download it from iCloud through Apple's Photos framework (PhotoKit). iOS also syncs your photo library with iCloud, including changes you make in WakeTok. This happens between your device and Apple; WakeTok does not send any data to us or anyone else.

Changes WakeTok makes to your photo library

WakeTok changes your photo library only when you ask it to:

  • Mark or unmark an item as a Favourite.
  • Add an item to an existing album.
  • Add an item to the "WakeTok" album with a left swipe. WakeTok creates this album in Photos the first time you add an item, not when the app opens; with full access it reuses an existing "WakeTok" album. With limited access WakeTok cannot see your existing albums, so after a fresh install it creates a new "WakeTok" album. Nothing is moved or deleted.
  • Delete an item. iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos.

Sharing

When you share an item, it goes through the iOS share sheet to the app or person you choose; we do not receive a copy. Photos are shared without their location information; Live Photos are shared as a still photo. Videos are shared as the original file, which keeps its original metadata, including the location where it was recorded, if present.

Data stored on your device

WakeTok stores a small amount of data on your device only:

  • Saved Pools — the names you give them and references to the albums they include — in a local database (SQLite).
  • App settings and state — your switches, first-launch tip progress, and the identifier of the WakeTok album — in local preferences.

WakeTok does not send this data anywhere. Like other app data, it is included in your iOS device backups if you use them. Deleting WakeTok removes this data; your photos and the WakeTok album stay in Photos.

While you browse, WakeTok writes a few temporary image files to its temporary folder on your device so photos can be shown quickly; iOS clears that folder from time to time and does not include it in backups. When you share a photo, WakeTok makes a temporary copy without location for the share sheet and deletes it afterwards.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub, as the host, receives technical data such as your IP address, under GitHub's own privacy statement. This site adds no analytics or cookies of its own.

Platforms and changes

This policy covers WakeTok for iOS. If WakeTok becomes available on other platforms, or this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.
